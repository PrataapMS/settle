Response contract

Every field in the current output has a home in Settle, plus the decision-specific parts it lacked: options, a weighted matrix, gates and per-turn deltas. The current output also has six defects. Settle fixes them in the engine rather than in the prompt, because a prompt cannot guarantee consistency.

Who does what

Jev · System 1 · every turn

Fast typed judgements

  • Turn check: picks one of seven routes with a calibrated probability, before any reasoning runs.
  • Record guard: yes or no per field after Claude replies, such as "Is this blocking unknown about the current decision?" This catches topic bleed.
  • Intuition check (optional): rent, buy or unsure from the record. Shown beside the matrix; disagreement prompts you to look for an unstated factor and never overrides.

Claude · System 2 · decision turns

Reasoning and writing

  • Reply prose, claim and direction.
  • Matrix scores with their basis, input values for gates, assumptions and break conditions.
  • Stress tests, next actions and the prediction to check.

Engine · plain code · every decision turn

Numbers nobody asserts

  • Totals, gate status, evidence strength, ceiling, deltas.
  • Invariant checks; a failure rejects the turn.
  • Writes state v+1 and the hash link.

Engine computes (never taken from the model)

FieldRule
Weighted totalsSum of weight × score ÷ 5 per option; weights must sum to 100.
Gate statusValue against threshold: pass, fail, partial, or unknown when the value is null.
Evidence strengthInputs known ÷ required × source weight (self-report 0.8, document 1.0).
CeilingPrepare if protective gates pass, risk ≤ 50 and executable; Simulate if 3+ inputs known; else Recommend. Request go-ahead is always yours.
System confidenceStable when confidence moved under 15 points since the last state; else Evolving.
DeltasEvery score, total, gate and the ceiling against the previous state.
InvariantsWeights, score ranges, gate completeness, single-source risk, chain link. Any break rejects the turn.
